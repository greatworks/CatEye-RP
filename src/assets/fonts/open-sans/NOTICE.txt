Unmodified Open Sans version 1.10, ten normal and italic faces:
Light (300), Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800),
and their italic counterparts. The 400 italic face is OpenSans-Italic.ttf;
the retained OpenSans-RegularItalic.ttf is byte-identical and is not duplicated.

These bytes were obtained from the Fonts directory of the locally retained
Axure RP 11.0.0.4150 distribution. Their embedded name records identify the
copyright holders and Apache 2.0 license. These fonts are separately licensed
third-party font software, not a license to Axure application code or assets.
They are not claimed byte-identical to a current upstream release.
